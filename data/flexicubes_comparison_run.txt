A. Extraction accuracy at K=2, fixed field, matched grids
==============================================================================
   Error is distance to the analytic surface in grid cells, scored two
   ways: over points sampled uniformly by area, and over the extracted
   vertices. The sampled figure is the comparable one. Our vertices are
   by construction zeros of the interpolant along tet edges, whereas a
   dual vertex is an average of its cell's crossings and is not meant to
   lie on the surface at all, so the vertex figure flatters us.
   FlexiCubes runs float32 with default weights; ours runs float64.

==============================================================================
C. A triple junction, at K=3
==============================================================================
   Standard double bubble: two equal spheres a radius apart,
   three regions meeting along a circle. FlexiCubes takes one scalar
   field, so each region is extracted one-vs-rest and the results
   are asked whether they form a partition.

   Overlap is the fraction of the domain two regions both claim,
   which is zero for anything that is a partition. Swept over
   resolution, because a defect that refines away is a different
   complaint from one that does not.

   extractor also loses when run one-vs-rest is the decomposition's
   doing and not theirs.
